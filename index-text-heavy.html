About Pending Circus

Pending Circus is a duo performance project led by Sarah and John. Our work blends dance, immersive character performance, acrobatics, theatrical atmosphere, and live improvisation.

We create experiences that can be elegant, strange, romantic, funny, dreamlike, or visually bold depending on the event or production. We are especially drawn to immersive environments, themed worlds, and performances that invite genuine audience connection.
