Print notes

The stock

Any plain 16pt card works. Ask for a matte or soft touch finish so the dark front does not show fingerprints. No foil, no white ink, nothing special to confirm with the shop.

What to tell the printer

  • Trim size 3.5″ × 2″, bleed 0.125″ all sides, safe margin 0.15″.
  • Both sides print full colour. The front gray floods to the bleed edge.
  • Ask for one physical proof before the full run. The gray and the peach shift a little from screen to paper.

Getting it to them

Open this page and print to PDF at 100% scale (turn off "fit to page"), or send the printer this HTML with the specs above. Most shops take a PDF. The trim and bleed numbers matter more than the file format.

Quantity

Order 100. The copy on the back will change as the work does, and a card is better handed to someone than left in a stack.
