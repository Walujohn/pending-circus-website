Print notes

The stock

The back is the business model in three lines: who we are with an audience, what we are together, and what we own and run. If a lane ever comes off the card, take the third one off last — the mirror suit, the stilts and the sewing machine are the things a competitor can't just decide to have.

Ask for mirror foil or silver metallic laminate card, 16–18pt, printed one or two sides. On mirror stock the shine is the paper, not the ink — so the design has to leave the metal exposed. That's why the back is mostly open: every dotted leader and blank inch is mirror.

What to tell the printer

  • Trim size 3.5″ × 2″, bleed 0.125″ all sides, safe margin 0.15″.
  • Front prints white ink + full-colour photo; white ink is not optional on mirror stock — without it, light areas go transparent and vanish into the metal. Confirm the shop can lay down white.
  • Back prints black only.
  • Ask for a physical proof before the full run. Foil shifts colour badly on screen.

Getting it to them

Open this page and print to PDF at 100% scale (turn off "fit to page"), or send the printer this HTML and the specs above. If they want a native file, most shops accept a PDF; the important thing is the trim and bleed measurements, not the format.

The one risk

Mirror stock makes small text hard to read at an angle. The contact line and the QR sit on solid ink for exactly that reason — don't move them onto bare foil to make it prettier, or the card becomes beautiful and useless.

Quantity

Order 100, not 500. You will change the rates after the first ten conversations, and a mirror card is worth handing out deliberately rather than by the fistful.
