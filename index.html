About

✶ ✶ ✶

Sarah is a professional dancer, choreographer and instructor and has danced since she was three. John came to one of her classes as a student and stayed on as a performing partner.

Together they have performed at galas, private parties, clubs, festivals and stage productions, and they design and build their own costumes. Based in Atlanta, available to travel.
