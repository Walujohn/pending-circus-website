What We Do

✶ ✶ ✶

Three things, and we do all three ourselves.

One

Characters & Kids

Bringing a character to life is the part that's hard to hire for — the improvising, the staying in it, the kid who won't let go of your hand. That's what we're good at. Tell us your theme and we'll build or build out a costume for it, then show up in character with games, stories, and photo time.

For birthdays, libraries, schools, festivals, hospitals, and family days.
Two

Duo Performance

Two performers who work together, not two acts sharing a calendar. Paired characters, ambient walkaround, partner lifts, dance and movement — trained, rehearsed, and improvised live when the room asks for something else.

For galas, openings, corporate events, festivals, and productions.

"Your talent and chemistry as a duo really shone through every movement." — Guyesha Blackshear, Internal Events Contractor, OpenGov

Three

Costumes, Gear & Production

The mirror suit and the stilt costumes are ours — premium pieces most performers rent, if they can get them at all. Sarah sews, so a costume can be made or altered for your theme. And we self-manage: we bring it, run it, strike it, and produce whole events ourselves, including our own — like the Doggie Regency Ball coming to Atlanta in 2027.

For producers, other performers, and anyone who needs the whole thing handled.

"Thank you again for making our after party so memorable. We're already thinking about future events where we can work together again." — Guyesha Blackshear, Internal Events Contractor, OpenGov

We Make the Costumes

✶ ✶ ✶

Some start as a drawing. Some start as something we take apart and rebuild. Either way it gets made here — so the character can be whatever your event needs.

About

✶ ✶ ✶

Pending Circus is Sarah, John, and friends — they met in a dance class and never looked back.

Sarah has danced since she was three, and works as a professional dancer, choreographer, and instructor. John came as a student and stayed as a co-creator. For two years they've performed constantly — galas, parties, clubs, festivals, and productions — sewing, rebuilding, and hauling their own costumes the whole way.

Based in Atlanta. Available everywhere.
