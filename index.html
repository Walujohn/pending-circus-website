About

✶ ✶ ✶

Pending Circus is Sarah, John, and friends — they met in a dance class and never looked back.

Sarah has been dancing since she was three years old, starting with ballet, and has spent her life as a professional dancer, choreographer, instructor and performer. John came as a student and stayed as a co-creator. For the past two years they have been performing constantly — galas, parties, clubs, productions, and everywhere in between.

Sarah designs and builds our costumes in-house, and we manage, transport, and run our own equipment — you book us, we handle the rest.

Based in Atlanta. Available everywhere.
