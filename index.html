About Pending Circus

Pending Circus blends dance, acrobatics, atmosphere, and visual storytelling into performance that feels polished on arrival and unforgettable in photos. We are at our best when an event needs more than filler: a dramatic welcome, a visual anchor, a stylish roaming presence, or a performance moment that changes the energy in the room.

We work comfortably with mixed-age audiences, are warm with guests, and can incorporate light sign language into interaction in ways that add accessibility, ease, and human connection without losing the atmosphere.
