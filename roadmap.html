Internal roadmap

Pending Circus milestone plan.

Pending Circus is a duo performance project led by Sarah and John. The focus now is to simplify the brand, build around strong visual offerings, keep taking real gigs, improve business knowledge, grow proof materials, and create more freedom for future travel, risk-taking, and bigger opportunities.

Main direction

Clear offerings, stronger visuals, real-world booking experience, better systems, and steady outreach.

Longer-term aim

Build a business and body of work strong enough to support travel, creative risk, and more ambitious opportunities over time.

Milestones

Focus on momentum over perfection. The goal is to make the business clearer, more visual, and more usable while continuing to work.

Milestone 1: tighten the website

Simplify the site so it is more visual, less text-heavy, and easier to understand quickly.

  • Lead with photos and short video clips
  • Keep the copy short and direct
  • Show core offerings clearly: lollipop, mirror suit, duo dance, themed entertainment
  • Use real event photos and clips as they come in

Milestone 2: build the portfolio through real work

Keep taking gigs, building relationships, and collecting stronger proof.

  • Book more lollipop gigs
  • Book more mirror suit and ambient performance gigs
  • Keep developing duo dance material
  • Prioritize professional photos and video whenever possible

Milestone 3: improve the physical toolkit

Invest in tools that support booking and visual quality without losing focus.

  • Research purchasing a lollipop apparatus
  • Research purchasing or creating a mirror suit
  • Budget for costume upgrades, repairs, and transport
  • Choose purchases that help land more gigs and better visuals

Milestone 4: grow the proof materials

Turn gig work into a stronger media package for future outreach.

  • Create a short reel from real footage
  • Build a small lookbook or gallery deck
  • Refresh the website with stronger media every month or two
  • Organize footage by category so it is easy to reuse

Milestone 5: develop the Bridgerton Dog Adoption Ball on the side

Build this slowly as a themed event concept while the gig side keeps moving.

  • Refine the concept and visual direction
  • Create a simple event page
  • Collect inspiration and early imagery
  • Think of it as a repeatable themed experience package

Milestone 6: organize the business basics

Keep the admin side simple and usable while improving business understanding.

  • Track income and expenses
  • Track taxes and subscriptions
  • Track renewals and LLC items
  • Add performance insurance into the budget
  • Learn the basics of pricing, margin, and event profitability
  • Track which gigs are worth repeating and which are not
  • Build a simple travel-cost planning habit for future out-of-state work
  • Keep notes on what kinds of opportunities would support more freedom and mobility later
  • Create a clearer picture of monthly personal and business cash needs before taking bigger risks

Milestone 7: start focused outreach

Use the better visuals and clearer offerings to reach out consistently.

  • Target corporate event opportunities
  • Target larger productions and creative collaborators
  • Keep outreach simple and visual
  • Send website, reel, and photos instead of long explanations
  • Build confidence reaching outside your current local circle
  • Include opportunities that could support travel in the future
  • Stay open to bolder asks and more ambitious connections over time
  • Use outreach as a way to expand possibility, not just fill the next date
  • Track which types of outreach could eventually lead to more adventurous or location-flexible work

Milestone 8: keep training while building

Let the gigs, training, and media growth reinforce each other.

  • Keep improving dance and partner work
  • Keep refining visual polish
  • Use each event as practice, proof, and relationship-building
  • Keep moving toward bigger opportunities without abandoning current income
The aim is not to do everything at once. The aim is to keep building a business that becomes clearer, stronger, and more flexible — one that can support better work, more travel, and larger creative risks over time.
