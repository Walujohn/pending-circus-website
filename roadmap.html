Internal roadmap

Pending Circus roadmap.

Pending Circus is a duo performance project led by Sarah and John, specializing in immersive character work, movement-based storytelling, thematic ballroom-cirque performance, and live audience improvisation. Our work blends dance, elegance, humor, theatrical atmosphere, and acrobatic physicality. We are especially strong in world-building performance for immersive events, fantasy environments, and story-led live experiences.

Focus

The goal is not to become a general gig company. The goal is to build a clear artistic identity, stronger proof materials, simple business systems, repeatable themed experience packages, and a consistent outreach rhythm that supports bigger opportunities over time.

Guiding principle

Keep the main effort on artistic positioning, proof, systems, and outreach. Keep side-income tools useful, but do not let them define the brand.

Phase 1 — clarify the artistic identity

Build a language people can understand and remember.

Milestone 1: finalize the core brand statement

Create one polished paragraph, one short version, and one sentence version.

Starter copy

Pending Circus is a duo performance project led by Sarah and John, specializing in immersive character work, movement-based storytelling, thematic ballroom-cirque performance, and live audience improvisation. Our work blends elegance, humor, theatrical atmosphere, and acrobatic physicality. We are especially strong in world-building performance for immersive events, fantasy environments, and story-led live experiences.

Short version

Pending Circus creates immersive duo performance blending character, dance, acrobatics, and live audience connection.

One-line version

Immersive duo performance for story-led live experiences.

Milestone 2: define three repeatable performance worlds

Choose three artistic lanes that feel aligned, castable, and expandable.

Immersive fantasy characters
Theatrical romance and cinematic duets
Playful live spectacle with audience interaction
Starter section copy

Pending Circus can be elegant, comic, romantic, eerie, dreamy, or fantastical depending on the world we are helping build.

Milestone 3: define repeatable themed experience packages

Start with experience packages, not unrelated physical products.

  • One immersive welcome package
  • One theatrical duet package
  • One audience-interaction package
Starter naming prompt

Build package names around world, feeling, and guest experience rather than props or apparatus.

Starter description prompt

Each package should answer: who are we, what world are guests entering, what do they feel, and what can we deliver in 5, 20, or 60 minutes.

Phase 2 — build the proof materials

Make it easy for someone to understand the work quickly.

Milestone 4: publish the website as the home base

Keep it clean, visual, and simple. Use placeholders until better photos are ready.

  • Homepage with clear positioning
  • Three performance worlds
  • Selected gallery
  • Short about section
  • Simple contact path

Milestone 5: replace placeholders with proof

Gather images and clips that show real chemistry, world-building, and audience response.

One strong hero image
Three gallery images minimum
At least one image of immersive character work
At least one image of dance or theatrical elegance
At least one image showing duo chemistry or audience interaction

Milestone 6: create a simple lookbook

Make one polished asset you can send with outreach.

  • 6 to 10 best photos
  • Brand statement
  • Three performance worlds
  • Contact information
  • Website and Instagram links

Milestone 7: assemble a short reel

Aim for a concise reel that quickly communicates vibe, world, and capability.

Starter sequence prompt

Lead with the strongest visual world first, then duo chemistry, then movement range, then audience-facing moments.

Phase 3 — create the business system

Get the basics out of your head and into simple structures.

Milestone 8: organize the LLC operations

Create a place to track taxes, subscriptions, renewals, budget, and insurance.

  • Tax tracking spreadsheet
  • Renewals and compliance spreadsheet
  • Subscriptions tracker
  • Budget tracker
  • Performance insurance line item

Milestone 9: create a lightweight budget

Budget for clarity, not perfection.

Website and domain
Insurance
Costumes and repairs
Travel and outreach
Marketing materials
Training or rehearsal costs

Milestone 10: plan new marketing materials before year-end

Schedule a focused refresh instead of endlessly tweaking.

  • Logo direction
  • Updated images
  • Reel refresh
  • Lookbook revision
  • Website image refresh

Phase 4 — build the opportunity pipeline

Use a consistent outreach system rather than random bursts of effort.

Milestone 11: build a target list

Create a broad list with room for local, national, and international opportunities.

  • Name
  • Type
  • Why they fit
  • Contact method
  • Date contacted
  • Next follow-up date

Milestone 12: write a simple outreach template

Keep it human, short, and easy to personalize.

Starter outreach structure

Brief introduction. One sentence on what Pending Circus is. One sentence on why your work may fit their world. Offer a website, reel, or lookbook link. Close warmly and simply.

Milestone 13: start a focused outreach rhythm

Small consistency beats rare intensity.

Add new targets weekly
Send a small number of thoughtful messages weekly
Track follow-ups
Refine copy based on responses

Weekly rhythm

Keep progress steady without letting the business consume every hour.

Website lane

One section, one image, one link, or one copy improvement.

Business lane

One admin task, one budget update, one tracker update, or one outreach action.

Art lane

Rehearsal, concept development, costume work, filming, or movement practice.

The aim is not to do everything at once. The aim is to build a clear identity, a believable body of proof, simple systems, and a steady path toward larger artistic opportunities.
