About the event

This event is designed as a charming, theatrical, and deeply lovable fundraiser: one part social dance, one part immersive costume experience, and one part dog adoption celebration.

Bridgerton Dog Adoptions brings together performance, community, and compassion. Guests can enjoy a visually rich Regency-inspired atmosphere while meeting adoptable dogs styled as miniature nobles, sweet scoundrels, dramatic debutantes, and dashing dukes.
