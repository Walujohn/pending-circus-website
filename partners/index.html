What you get

  • Four or more dates a year at a package rate below our per-event pricing.
  • Priority on our calendar — your dates are held before we take outside work.
  • One contract, one invoice, one certificate of insurance. Not four of each.
  • A short planning call each season: you name the theme, we build or alter a costume for it.
  • Photos from every event, sent to you to use.

Why us

  • We're good with kids and good in a crowd. Bringing a character to life and holding it for two hours is the part that's hard to hire for.
  • We're a duo, not a roster. The same two people show up every time, and your audience gets to know them.
  • We make and modify our own costumes. Sarah sews, so a character can be built or altered for your theme instead of whatever a rental company has in stock.
  • We own premium gear. The mirror suit and stilt costumes are ours — pieces most performers can't get.
  • We self-manage. We bring, run, and strike everything. Nobody on your staff has to produce us.
